Dog Boarding

Best dog boarding services in Los Angeles County, CA.

Boarding with us isn't a kennel holding pattern. Your dog stays in a structured, supervised routine where the habits you've worked on keep getting reinforced — feeding schedules, calm crate time, leash manners and daily handling — so you don't come home to a dog that's forgotten everything.

  • Supervised care with a consistent daily routine
  • Training reinforced every day of the stay

Puppy Training

Trained right from day one.

The easiest behavior problem to fix is the one that never forms. We start puppies on the fundamentals — name response, potty and crate routines, polite greetings, bite inhibition, leash introduction and confident socialization — so the dog they grow into is the dog you hoped for.

  • House manners, crate and potty routines
  • Calm greetings and gentle mouth manners
  • Early socialization with people, dogs and noise

On-Leash Dog Training

The obedience that makes daily life easy.

This is the core program most families need: loose-leash walking without pulling, a reliable sit, down and stay, a recall that works, and calm behavior around other dogs, strangers, bikes and doorbells. We train it in the places you actually walk your dog.

  • No more pulling, lunging or zig-zag walks
  • Sit, down, stay, place and recall under distraction
  • Handler coaching so results hold at home

Advanced Off-Leash Training

Freedom, with total control.

Off-leash reliability is earned, not guessed at. We proof every command against escalating real-world distractions — other dogs, wildlife, open space, traffic noise — until your dog responds the first time, whether the leash is attached or not. Perfect for hikes, parks and unfenced yards.

  • Rock-solid recall from distance and distraction
  • Duration stays and boundary awareness
  • Trained in real environments, not a practice ring

Custom Dog Training Programs

We don't rely on one-size-fits-all advice.

Some dogs need something built from scratch — reactivity around other dogs, separation stress, resource guarding, door-dashing, a rescue with an unknown history, or a specific goal your family cares about. We assess the dog in front of us and design the plan around them, with unlimited in-person custom training sessions.

  • Plan shaped by breed, age and temperament
  • In-home sessions or group classes — your call
  • Lifetime support after the program ends
