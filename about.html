About Us

We train dogs, not just behaviors

We've been helping dogs and families thrive since 1992. Our school is family-run, backed by over 30 years of hands-on training experience.

Our Story

Three decades of real dogs, real families

Smart Dog Training School started in 1992 and has been family-run ever since. In that time we've trained and boarded dogs of every breed and size across Los Angeles County — puppies taking their first walk, rescues learning to trust, and grown dogs whose families were close to giving up.

We train and board dogs of all breeds with lifetime support. From puppy basics to advanced behavior, we make life easier for dogs and their humans. We offer in-home sessions, group classes, and the best dog boarding services in Los Angeles County, CA.

The approach hasn't changed because it works: watch the dog in front of you, understand what's actually driving the behavior, then teach both ends of the leash how to fix it.

Our Approach

Real training. Real dogs. Real lasting results.

With 30 years of proven experience, we work with all breeds — large or small — bringing calm, confidence and clarity to every dog through our in-person programs and care.

We read the dog first

Every plan starts with an honest assessment — breed drives, age, history, temperament and what's really triggering the behavior.

No one-size-fits-all advice

Generic programs create generic results. Yours is built around your dog, your home and the life you actually live.

Family-run, hands-on

You're not passed between staff. The same people who take your call are the ones working with your dog.

Lifetime support

Dogs change as they age. We stay reachable for the life of your dog — not just for the length of a package.

Calm, not compliance

A confident dog behaves because it understands what's expected — not because it's afraid to get it wrong.

All across LA County

In-home sessions, group classes and boarding, with free pickup and delivery throughout Los Angeles County.
